# Visual & Video Master Prompts: Episode 2

These prompts are engineered for **Zero-Rewrite Production**:
Each prompt is generated **once in 16:9 widescreen**, with the primary action concentrated in the **central vertical column**. This allows the exact same footage to be used for:
1. **YouTube Long-Form Video** (16:9 widescreen cinematic)
2. **YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels** (9:16 vertical center crop or blurred-backdrop framing)

## 🎬 Master Prompt Library (Mapped to Narration Timeline)

### 🎬 C01 — The Arena of Five
- **Timeline**: `0:00 – 0:08` (Beat 1 Opening & Short #1 Hook)
- **Voiceover Context**: *"In Episode 1, we proved that reciprocal trust can survive against raw predation... What happens when you drop multiple competing personalities into a room together?"*

#### Prompt (16:9 with Center-Weighted 9:16 Safe Zone):
> Create an 8-second cinematic 16:9 widescreen science-fiction video for a technical documentary about a multi-agent game theory experiment.
>
> Opening shot: a vast dark computational laboratory is almost completely hidden in atmospheric blackness and blue mist.
>
> The camera slowly pushes forward.
>
> As the camera approaches, five sleek minimalist humanoid robotic agents with smooth faceless glowing cube heads gradually become visible standing in a circular arena.
>
> Each represents a different strategic personality:
> - One agent glows calm cyan, representing cooperation.
> - One glows sharp amber, representing predation.
> - One glows cyan-teal with a thin reciprocal connection, representing Tit-for-Tat.
> - One glows deep magenta, representing an unforgiving Grudger.
> - One glows unstable violet with subtle irregular flickering, representing a random Chaos agent.
>
> Thin cyan and amber fiber-optic connections form between the agents. Polished black obsidian floor with subtle computational grid lines and reflections.
>
> The camera performs a slow dramatic cinematic dolly toward the center of the arena. The five agents remain mostly stationary while subtle energy pulses travel between them.
>
> **Framing Requirement**: The important five-agent formation must remain concentrated around the center of the frame so the footage can later be cropped to 9:16 for YouTube Shorts.
>
> Mysterious, intelligent, premium realistic 3D science-fiction aesthetic, volumetric haze, dramatic cyan, amber, magenta and violet lighting. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.

### 🎬 C02 — The Grudger's Fatal Wall

#### Prompt (16:9 with Center-Weighted 9:16 Safe Zone):
> Create a 15-second cinematic 16:9 widescreen science-fiction video.
>
> Inside a dark futuristic computational chamber, two minimalist humanoid robots with smooth glowing cube heads face each other.
>
> A cyan robot represents a cooperative agent. Opposite it stands a deep magenta robot representing an unforgiving Grim Trigger strategy.
>
> At first, a thin cyan communication filament connects the two robots. A brief amber electrical disturbance travels through the connection, representing a single unexpected betrayal.
>
> Immediately afterward, the magenta robot generates a massive jagged amber computational firewall between them. The firewall rapidly expands upward and becomes an imposing permanent barrier.
>
> The cyan robot attempts to send a calm cyan signal through the connection. The signal strikes the amber wall and shatters into tiny glowing particles. The magenta robot remains completely still behind the barrier, refusing to reconnect.
>
> Camera movement: slow cinematic tracking movement toward the growing firewall, ending with the barrier dominating the center of the frame.
>
> **Framing Requirement**: The robots and barrier must remain concentrated around the central vertical area of the composition so the footage can also be cropped to 9:16 for a Short.
>
> Dark obsidian surfaces, atmospheric blue mist, dramatic amber and cyan lighting, volumetric fog, realistic reflections, premium cinematic 3D science-fiction aesthetic. Slow restrained movement, no walking, no transformations. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.

### 🎬 C03 — The Cooperative Society
- **Timeline**: `2:15 – 2:25` (Beat 3 Climax & Short #3 Payoff)
- **Voiceover Context**: *"You do not have to beat your opponent to succeed... By cultivating mutual cooperation, reciprocal agents generate massive collective wealth."*

#### Prompt (16:9 with Center-Weighted 9:16 Safe Zone):
> Create a 10-second epic cinematic 16:9 widescreen science-fiction video.
>
> Begin with several sleek minimalist robotic agents standing separately in a dark computational environment. Each robot has a smooth faceless cube head. The agents initially appear disconnected and isolated.
>
> One cyan light filament forms between two cooperative agents. A pulse of cyan light travels through the connection. Additional cyan filaments rapidly but naturally connect more agents.
>
> The camera slowly pulls backward and upward, revealing that the small group is part of a much larger interconnected digital society.
>
> Dozens of cyan and teal robotic agents become visible across elevated translucent computational platforms. Continuous streams of cyan light travel throughout the network like a living neural system.
>
> Far below the illuminated network, a few isolated amber robotic figures remain trapped in darkness, disconnected from the society above.
>
> The visual progression communicates: individual cooperation → repeated cooperation → interconnected society.
>
> Camera movement: slow majestic pull-back and upward reveal.
>
> **Framing Requirement**: Keep the main illuminated network concentrated around the center of the frame so it remains usable when cropped vertically to 9:16.
>
> Epic scale, dark cinematic atmosphere, volumetric light rays, polished obsidian and translucent computational architecture, premium realistic 3D science-fiction aesthetic. No text, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.

### 🎬 C04 — The Glitch in the Wire (Noise & Episode 3 Teaser)
- **Timeline**: `2:38 – 2:50` (Beat 4 Outro & Episode 3 Teaser)
- **Voiceover Context**: *"What happens if an agent wants to cooperate, but a 2% glitch in the wire flips their action to betrayal? In Episode 3, we introduce Noise..."*

#### Prompt (16:9 with Center-Weighted 9:16 Safe Zone):
> Create a 12-second cinematic science-fiction video designed for both 16:9 YouTube and later 9:16 vertical cropping.
>
> A single luminous cyan fiber-optic communication cable runs vertically through the center of a dark computational environment. A clean cyan signal travels smoothly through the cable. The surrounding environment is almost completely black with subtle atmospheric haze.
>
> After several seconds of clean communication, the signal suddenly encounters a tiny digital disturbance. The cyan signal flickers.
>
> The disturbance rapidly grows into violent red, orange and amber digital corruption. The clean signal fractures into jagged electrical patterns and glowing pixel fragments. Tiny particles scatter into the darkness.
>
> The corruption spreads briefly along the cable before stabilizing into an unstable broken signal.
>
> Camera movement: extremely slow cinematic push toward the corrupted section. At the moment of failure, introduce a very subtle camera micro-jitter and rapid electrical flicker.
>
> **Framing Requirement**: The cable must remain precisely in the central vertical region so the same footage can be cropped safely into a 9:16 YouTube Short.
>
> Extreme shallow depth of field, macro cinematic photography, realistic electrical detail, dark computational environment, dramatic cyan versus red/amber contrast. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
