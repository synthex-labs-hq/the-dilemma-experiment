# AI Visual & Concept Art Generation Prompts: Episode 2

This guide contains ready-to-copy prompts tailored for:
1. **Google Gemini (Imagen 3) & ChatGPT (DALL-E 3)** — Natural-language descriptive prompts optimized for LLM image generators (no tag stuffing, clean composition, zero text glitches).
2. **Leonardo AI & Midjourney** — Token-weighted prompts with negative prompt blocks.
3. **Video Generators (PixVerse, Hailuo AI, Kling)** — Motion animation direction prompts.

> **Visual Identity Anchor**: Dark, minimalist computational laboratory, obsidian reflective floors, glowing minimalist humanoid figures with clean cube heads, cyan/teal neon (cooperation) vs amber/red electrical sparks (predation/defection) vs magenta/purple (grudger/grim trigger).

## Scene 1: The Arena of Five (Hero Cover & YouTube Thumbnail)
*Role: Episode 2 Thumbnail & Beat 1 Opening Visual*

### For Google Gemini & ChatGPT (DALL-E 3):
> A cinematic 3D digital concept art illustration of five stylized humanoid robotic figures standing in a circle inside a vast, dark computational laboratory. The figures are sleek and minimalist, each with a smooth, faceless glowing cube for a head. The floor is polished, reflective dark obsidian glass with faint glowing grid lines underneath. Two of the figures emit a gentle, luminous cyan and teal neon glow, with glowing fiber-optic light threads connecting them. Two predatory figures on the opposite side pulse with sharp amber and deep orange electrical embers. A fifth figure glows in rich magenta. Dramatic volumetric lighting, deep shadows, atmospheric haze, futuristic cyberpunk minimalism. Wide 16:9 cinematic shot with generous empty space at the top and bottom for text overlays. No letters, words, or text anywhere in the image.

### For Leonardo AI & Midjourney:
> **Prompt**: Cinematic wide-angle shot of five glowing humanoid robotic entities with minimalist cube heads standing in a circular digital arena. Inside a vast dark computational laboratory. Floor made of reflective dark obsidian glass with faint cyan and amber grid lines. Volumetric lighting, ethereal neon glow, cyan light connecting cooperative entities, amber electrical sparks emitting from predatory figures. High contrast, clean composition, room at top and bottom for text overlay, octane render, 8k, cinematic depth of field.  
> **Negative Prompt**: messy, chaotic, cluttered, text, words, labels, watermarks, deformed limbs, blurry, low resolution, realistic human faces, bright daytime lighting.

- **Motion 3 (The Society Network)**:
