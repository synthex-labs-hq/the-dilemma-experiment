# AI Visual & Concept Art Generation Prompts: Episode 2

Use these prompts in **Leonardo AI**, **Midjourney**, **PixVerse**, or **Hailuo AI**.  
They are tailored to maintain visual consistency with Episode 1's dark cybernetic laboratory aesthetic.

> **Pro-Tip for Video Generators (PixVerse / Hailuo / Kling):**  
> Generate images in **16:9** or **9:16** with ample negative space at the top and bottom so text captions and cropping never obstruct the subjects.

---

### Prompt 1: The Arena of Five (Hero Cover & YouTube Thumbnail)
- **Role**: Episode 2 Thumbnail & Beat 1 Visual (`ai_clip_01_arena`)
- **Prompt**:
  > Cinematic wide-angle shot of five glowing humanoid robotic entities with minimalist cube heads standing in a circular digital arena. Inside a vast dark computational laboratory. Floor made of reflective dark obsidian glass with faint cyan and amber grid lines. Volumetric lighting, ethereal neon glow, cyan light connecting cooperative entities, amber electrical sparks emitting from predatory figures. High contrast, clean composition, room at top and bottom for text overlay, octane render, 8k, cinematic depth of field.
- **Negative Prompt**:
  > messy, chaotic, cluttered, text, watermarks, deformed limbs, blurry, low resolution, oversaturated bright day lighting.

### Prompt 2: The Grudger's Fatal Wall (Beat 2 Visual)
- **Role**: Beat 2 Visual (`ai_clip_02_grudger`)
- **Prompt**:
  > Cinematic medium shot of a single minimalist cube-headed robotic figure glowing in deep magenta neon, abruptly turning its back and erecting a jagged, glowing amber computational barrier. In the background, another glowing cube figure reaches out with a fading cyan pulse. Dark futuristic cybernetic server room, volumetric fog, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, emotional storytelling, cinematic composition.

  > messy, human skin, organic faces, text, watermarks, bright cartoon colors.

### Prompt 3: The Cooperative Society Network (Beat 3 Visual)
- **Role**: Beat 3 Visual (`ai_clip_03_cooperation`)
- **Prompt**:
  > Wide cinematic shot of a thriving digital society of dozens of glowing cube-headed cybernetic figures interconnected by brilliant cyan and teal fiber-optic light streams. They stand on elevated computational glass pedestals. In the lower shadowed crevices, isolated amber figures flicker in dim electrical isolation. Epic scale, sense of community and emergent civilization, sleek sci-fi minimalism, cinematic volumetric lighting, 8k resolution.

### Prompt 4: The Glitch in the Wire (Beat 4 & Episode 3 Teaser)
- **Role**: Beat 4 Teaser (`ai_clip_04_noise`)
